## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

First production release.

### Added

- **Logins**: create a login and sign in; a login is shared by a group, which owns its players and games. Change the
  password; admins come from the server configuration.
- **Players** with an avatar each, played in a chosen seat order.
- **Question bank**: shared categories and questions in Slovak and English, generated by AI, imported as JSON or
  written by hand (admins only). Answers are numbers.
- **Games** of 2–12 players: a random question the login has seen least, the "To je priveľa!" call, bad cards worth the
  question's or the chooser's bad points (1–5), doubles, a bad-card limit (2–10), finishing early and deleting a game.
- **Results, history and player statistics** (losses, loss index, calls, records).
- **Admin view of every login**: its players, games and statistics read only, and deleting a login with everything it
  owns (never your own, nor the first login, which holds the game's original data).
- **Versioning**: one version for the whole game in `VERSION`, reported by `GET /api/info` and shown in Settings.
